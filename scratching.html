Component 01 / Sound synthesis

Scratching synthesizer

A software model of turntable scratching based on record movement, sample position, playback velocity and cross-fader behavior.

Component owner: Michael Biggar

How it works

The instrument reads performance commands from the XML score and synthesizes scratching by moving through recorded sound.

Audio source

Recorded sample

CWaveLibrary provides audio data. The scratch instrument samples the recording at a continuously updated position instead of playing the full recording at normal speed.

Record movement

Motion model

The scratch controller determines a target playback velocity. Hand and motor parameters influence acceleration and the actual velocity used to move through the sample.

Cross-fader

Fader model

The fader opens and closes the audible signal. Its rate, open percentage, timing offset and response can shape short rhythmic cuts.

Scratch methods

The source code includes named configurations for five scratching methods. The final rubric level should be confirmed by the component owner.

Baby

Slower forward and backward movement without rapid fader cuts.

Scribble

Faster back-and-forth motion with more frequent cycles.

Chirp

Coordinated movement and short fader openings.

Transform

Repeating fader taps create rhythmic interruptions to the scratch audio.

Orbit

Forward and backward record motion forms alternating scratch strokes.

Motor simulation

Velocity blends motor-driven motion with hand-controlled motion using configurable pressure and response.

Score parameters

XML attributes configure performance per note. The program assigns defaults to parameters not included in a note.

Configurable controls

  • sample, method: recording and scratch type.
  • startPosition, speed, cycles, stroke: movement through the recording.
  • faderRate, faderOpenPercentage, faderOffset: rhythmic gating.
  • handPressure, motorSpeed, handResponse, motorResponse: physical motion behavior.
  • attack, release, gain: amplitude shaping.

Example note

<instrument instrument="ScratchInstrument">
  <note measure="2" beat="1"
        duration="1"
        sample="are-you-ready.wav"
        method="scribble"
        startPosition="0.38"
        speed="1.5" />
</instrument>

Individual grading rubric

The criteria below come from the course's scratching component page. Final grading depends on demonstrated behavior, not the presence of a parameter name.

PointsCriterion
10Audio playback on demand from the sequencer
20Attack and decay plus pitch variation
30Two scratch methods simulated
35Four scratch methods simulated
40General parameterization for scratch methods
50General method with a motor simulation
